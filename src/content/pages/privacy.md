## What we collect

The only signup on this site is the Watchlist waitlist, on the [Watchlist page](/watchlist/). Its form sends:

- the email address you type in (required)
- your brand's Instagram handle or website, if you fill it in
- up to five brands you want to watch, if you fill it in
- which list you joined (the Watchlist waitlist)

The form also has a hidden field that only spam bots fill in; it is empty when you submit.

With your signup we also record the time you signed up and the page you signed up from (its path, without any query string). We do not store your IP address with your signup, and there is no confirmation email to click.

## Why we collect it

We use your email address to send you one email when the Watchlist opens, and nothing before that. The handle or website and the brand list are optional; they tell us which brands waitlist members want to follow.

## Who stores it

Signups are stored by {emailProvider}, in a database table that is not publicly readable and that only this site's signup service writes to. See [Supabase's privacy policy](https://supabase.com/privacy).

## Cookies

This site does not set cookies and does not store anything in your browser. The fonts are served from this site, not from a font service.

## Analytics

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics, and no other analytics or third-party scripts. Its script is loaded from this site's own domain, and it reports page views to Vercel.

Vercel Web Analytics uses no third-party cookies and records page views anonymously. To tell visitors apart, it uses a hash of the incoming request, which is discarded after 24 hours. For each page view it stores the time, the URL, the referrer, filtered query parameters, your location to the city level, and your operating system, browser and device type. See [Vercel's Web Analytics privacy documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy).

## Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. As with any website, loading a page sends your browser's request, including your IP address, to the host.

Vercel processes that request data, including your IP address, to serve the site. See [Vercel's privacy notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice).

## Unsubscribe and deletion

To leave the waitlist, or to have your signup deleted (your address and anything you added in the optional fields), email {contactEmail}. We handle these requests within 30 days.

## About the posts we score

The scores on this site are commentary on brands' public social posts, collected from the brands' own accounts. We do not collect or publish anything about the people who like, comment on or are tagged in those posts.
